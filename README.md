This repo contains artefacts for a stroboscope I designed inspired by an exhibition I saw at the [Museum of Illusions Budapest](https://illuziokmuzeuma.hu/en/). I wanted to design a stroboscope with internal light and synchronisation such that no manual rotation speed or strobe frequency adjustment is required, and no strobe light or motor is visible.

The premise is that a geometrically designed translucent platter spins on an electric motor at a precise speed, synchronised using magnets in the platter and a hall effect sensor in the base. LED strips underneath the platter then strobe above the [flicker fusion threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flicker_fusion_threshold) of human eyesight, such that the geometry in the platter appears to morph and twist in an impossible manner.

In the off chance someone is actually interested in making this, or something similar, please get in contact and I will dig out a bill of materials I used in a final version.
Whilst this prototype does "work" (i.e. succeeds in the tricking the eye into seeting hovering shapes morphing and twisting), it could be executed far better with a higher RPM, quieter motor, and more attention to detail.
